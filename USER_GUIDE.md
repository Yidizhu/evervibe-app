# Using EverVibe

EverVibe helps two people turn the things they love into something to do, discuss and remember together. You can bring your own interests or explore four fictional couples' stories. The experience opens in English; choose English or Simplified Chinese in **Settings**.

## Try the interactive app

Open the [interactive app](https://evervibe.zesty-mesa-1831.chatgpt.site/try?lang=en). It has its own link, separate from the [four story walkthroughs](https://evervibe.zesty-mesa-1831.chatgpt.site/stories?lang=en). Each page keeps its own session progress.

A short, skippable welcome opens **Private chat with EVE**. Answer freely or skip; **Photo** opens a local photo preview. **Vibe → Let EVE collect them from our chat** prepares an editable introduction, stated partner wishes and catalog-verified interests from your own messages. Review the draft before using it. Your private transcript is never included in the candidate-facing preview.

1. **Open your black box.** In **My Vibe**, optionally set your age, gender and explicit partner criteria. Enable cultural discovery to compare your chosen tastes with Qloo. Open the full-screen box and drag or use the arrow keys to rotate it. Eight fictional adults are available, one introduction at a time. Both sides’ explicit criteria are checked; missing information is labeled exploratory. With cultural discovery enabled, distinct Qloo themes influence the ordering. No character has a verified nearby location, so a strict nearby-only requirement returns no demo introductions.
2. **Say hello.** The selected person’s name, portrait and authored voice follow you into direct chat and Make plans. Replies are clearly labeled simulated. Use **Another connection** to explore the remaining small pool; there is no public feed or infinite swiping. Relationship chats, memories and agreements remain separate. Returning to a relationship requires a fresh planning session before reusing recommendations.
3. **Share your tastes when ready.** Open **Settings** to search for a film, artist or book and add an actual catalog result to **Your Vibe**, up to three interests. You can also edit the selected companion’s sample Vibe and either person's note about today. Click **Use my location** and grant browser permission, or enter a city and country and select **Use this city**. The interactive app is not limited to the four story cities. Each person independently controls **Share my Vibe** and **Receive EVE suggestions**. Once both Vibes, location and all four participation controls are ready, select **Start together**. Settings closes and **Make plans** opens with Qloo's common cultural themes and an editable first question. Missing steps are explained beside the relevant settings.
4. **Try all three spaces.** Under **Chat**, use **Private chat with EVE** for personal advice, or **Chat with the companion** for the direct conversation. Each role has a separate private EVE thread. **Make plans** contains the shared Qloo-assisted conversation; in automatic mode, the companion contributes a clearly simulated response to your message before EVE helps with the plan. For example, you can ask for a relaxed afternoon with something new to discover. Manual role switching remains available in every conversation space.
5. **Choose a shared idea.** Ask EVE to help plan. Open **Why these suggestions?** to see the cultural starting points. Select a candidate and confirm from both roles. You can ask a follow-up about that choice without clearing either confirmation. Ask explicitly for new or different recommendations when you want to replace it.
6. **Keep a moment.** After both confirm a plan, **After our time together** opens a blank journal with the place as its title; it does not claim the outing happened. In **Our memories**, add a real-life moment yourself or let EVE prepare an editable excerpt from the shared conversation. Choose the date and words, propose the memory, then use **Review as the companion (demo)** (or **Review as Visitor**) to confirm it from the other role. Opening another memory entry point keeps an unfinished draft until you share or cancel it. Private chat is never used as a memory source.
7. **Grow the tree.** Each distinct, jointly saved memory earns one water and one nourishment. Use the two icons to care for the interactive 3D tree; the view brings the whole tree into sight for the response. It grows after one, three and six saved moments; the mature tree uses the same Tripo model and rose garden as the stories. These are visual demo milestones, not a relationship score.
8. **Make a small promise.** Open **An agreement for us** in Make plans. One person proposes; the other confirms. Editing a proposal requires renewed agreement. Manual memories and agreements remain available when EVE is off.

For a sample planning setup, open **Settings** and choose **Try two different tastes** while both Vibes are empty. It looks up *Knives Out* and *Amélie* and fills two sample Vibes without changing your location. Choose a location and review both people's sharing and EVE controls before selecting **Start together**; the shortcut does not enable them for you. If opening the selected companion’s box has already filled her Vibe, use the editable Vibes instead, or reset the session to try the sample shortcut.

the selected companion’s automatic replies are dialogue only. They never enable sharing or confirm a plan, memory or agreement. Review and confirm those actions yourself from both perspectives. A simulated “yes” in a message does not count as confirmation.

The black box introduces fictional characters, never real nearby users. Cultural comparisons are evidence of related interests, not proof of attraction or a predicted chance of a successful relationship.

Choosing a place saves an idea for this session. It does not book a table, buy a ticket or create an order.

You can ask private EVE for help before sharing your own Vibe. A partner's interests and mood note enter that request only when that partner has enabled sharing. Private transcripts are never forwarded to direct chat or shared planning. **Write to the companion** opens the direct composer without sending EVE's advice. Switching spaces, roles or interface language keeps messages and separate drafts during this page session. Closing a pending EVE window cancels that request; its message remains and can be retried. Try also offers local EVE check-ins when sharing starts and when a plan is confirmed. These are event-based prompts, not observations of real life.

Open the separate [four stories link](https://evervibe.zesty-mesa-1831.chatgpt.site/stories?lang=en). The chapter controls select a story, and the two names switch whose view you are experiencing. Only one person's view is shown at a time.

| Tokyo | Kenji and Anya | A photograph, a song and the curiosity to ask about them. |
| New York City | Chloe and Sarah | Different tastes become an outing with room to hear each other. |
| London | Emma and Arjun | Everyday kindness, a romantic gesture, and learning what each person actually needs. |
| Wellington | Leo and Julian | Film nights, photography and wandering conversations become a shared way of discovering the world. |

In Tokyo, open the black box and express interest as each person before their direct conversation begins. The characters, photographs and story events are fictional. They let you experience different moments in a relationship without needing a second person to be online.

## Three conversation spaces

**Private EVE chat** is where the current person can explore a thought, ask for help or find a way to begin. **Find the words** offers an optional story-line draft: it goes into the human's composer, and you choose whether to send it. Free-text EVE conversation is also available; arbitrary wording does not necessarily complete an authored story milestone.

**Direct chat** keeps EVE's guidance in the EVE windows. In the authored stories, switch roles to write each person's messages. In the interactive app, the selected companion’s clearly labeled simulated replies are on by default; turn them off in Settings to write both roles manually.

**Make plans** is the shared conversation with EVE. Each person can express what they want from the time together. EVE uses the shared interests and current wishes to suggest cultural possibilities. Both people choose whether an idea becomes their plan.

In both experiences, the common-ground card in **Make plans** shows each person's cultural starting points and Qloo's shared themes. These themes help shape the actual recommendation query; they are not a relationship score. Saved Qloo comparisons are labelled.

In the interactive app, choose **Use my location** to request browser permission, or enter a city and country, such as Hamilton, New Zealand. City input is not restricted to Tokyo, New York, London or Wellington. If permission is denied or a place cannot be resolved, enter or correct the city; the app does not silently substitute Tokyo. Device location uses approximate coordinates for nearby ideas and does not automatically name your city. The four authored stories keep their original settings: Tokyo, New York, London and Wellington.

## Sharing and EVE suggestions

Each role in both experiences has two settings:

- **Share my Vibe** makes that role's profile interests available for the relationship experience.
- **Receive EVE suggestions** allows that role to receive EVE's advice.

The shared EVE experience needs both connected people to participate. Later story chapters begin with sharing enabled as part of their fictional relationship; you can change the settings.

In the authored stories, with both settings enabled for both people, a small wish expressed in one person's private EVE chat may inspire a short EVE suggestion in the other person's private window. It is a separate suggestion, not a copy of the conversation or a message sent as the person. It does not appear in direct chat or shared planning. EVE may also offer an in-app check-in without waiting for a new question. Try's private advice uses the partner's explicitly shared Vibe; automatic delivery of free-form private wishes to the other role is not enabled there.

The implementation screens explicit confidentiality requests, sensitive details and surprises; it can return no partner suggestion. These checks do not guarantee that every expression will be understood correctly. Turning participation off stops new delivery; a suggestion already read cannot be made unseen.

## Plans and memories

After choosing a story plan, switch roles so each person can confirm it. **After our time together** then advances the fictional timeline. It does not detect a real outing.

In **Our memories**, each person chooses the words they want to keep. Review and confirm the shared entry from both roles. The memory tree gives those moments a place to return to. You can also add a moment yourself; the app does not infer that an event happened in real life.

In the authored stories, after both people share new messages with the shared EVE experience enabled, **EVE · Keep this moment?** offers **Review draft**. In the interactive app, **Help keep this moment** in **Our memories** prepares an editable excerpt from the participants' messages in **Make plans**. In automatic mode this can include the selected companion’s simulated dialogue; it is a demo conversation, not evidence of a real event. These drafts do not read private chats. You can edit the words, choose a date and propose the memory for both roles to confirm, or leave the draft aside. An unfinished personal draft is preserved. A distinct, jointly saved moment earns water and nourishment only once.

## Language and session progress

Use **Settings** to change the system language at any time. The interface and authored story text change without clearing the current conversation or actions. Existing typed messages and generated responses keep their original language; new EVE responses use the selected language. Qloo's official names and addresses are kept as returned.

The interactive app remembers chats, demo connections, sharing choices, memories, tree care, profile and drafts in this browser. Refresh or reopen the same link to continue. **Reset this device** clears that local save after confirmation. Clearing browser data or using private browsing may remove it. This is not cross-device account storage. Device coordinates are not saved; allow location again when needed. Saved plans must be refreshed before new confirmations. The authored story walkthrough remains a separate experience.

## If something is unavailable

- **No search result:** try another title or name. If the selected companion’s sample film cannot load, her profile and the link to the stories remain available; edit her sample Vibe or retry the search.
- **Expired idea details:** clear the choice and ask for fresh ideas. The conversation stays in place; an expired reference does not silently generate a replacement.
- **Expired interests:** remove the affected selections, search again and add fresh results.
- **A slow recommendation:** some cultural queries take longer than a title search. Keep the page open while EVE works, or use **Cancel response** to stop. A timeout keeps your conversation available for a manual retry.
- **An interrupted reply:** use **Try again** when available. You can cancel an in-progress response; your message remains in the conversation.
- **Anya cannot reply:** retry, or turn off automatic demo replies and switch perspectives to write her response yourself.
- **Location unavailable:** enter a city and country in Settings. If the place cannot be matched, add the country or try another name; no story city is chosen on your behalf.
- **No suitable recommendation or a planning service timeout:** use **Find cultural places** or **Find a film** when offered, or change the request or interests. The buttons express the activity type directly while keeping both Vibes and the same sharing and location checks. They appear only when there is no current set of plan candidates. The app does not invent a venue to fill the gap.
- **Live services unavailable:** authored stories remain viewable, but a service error is not a new Qloo result. A local installation needs server-side provider configuration as described in the [README](README.md).

This is a single-browser prototype, not a real multi-user dating service. It does not make purchases, reservations or external messages, and returned places do not confirm current availability or venue facilities. The app's session behavior does not determine the separate data-processing terms of its API providers or hosting service.

### Discover a bridge

After selecting both Vibes and enabling sharing, enter a city and select Start together directly. You do not need to press Use this city first. Make plans opens with shared themes when available and a new film supported by Qloo contributions from both Vibes. Open Why this bridge? to see the evidence boundary. Consider this together creates a proposed plan; review and confirm each perspective separately. Missing provider evidence has an explicit retry path and never becomes a made-up common interest.

## Personal exploration and independent connections

The mobile conversation keeps evaluation details in this guide. Fictional people retain a small Demo label. After three initial visitor messages, an optional invitation opens the black box; Stay with EVE dismisses it. For me provides Qloo film, music-artist and book recommendations from the visitor's selected verified interests alone. With no selected interests, My Vibe opens for review first. Private chat and partner data do not enter the solo Qloo request.

**People** switches between independent conversations and **Just me & EVE**. Each connection keeps separate chats, sharing choices, plans, agreements and memories. **Meet someone new** explores another fictional adult without removing earlier connections. Return to EVE to discuss what changed, then review and edit your Vibe before another introduction. Real-user profiles and bilateral discovery would require authenticated accounts and a protected backend; this prototype uses eight fictional adults.


## Updating your Vibe

Talk naturally with EVE, then choose Vibe → Let EVE collect them from our chat. Review the introduction, partner wishes and recognized favourites before applying. Confirmed profile facts and your own recent EVE messages help EVE remember you across your separate relationship spaces; other people’s private transcripts are never merged. Latest explicit corrections take precedence in the draft. Manual favourites remain optional under Add or change a favourite.

Discovery filters explicit age/gender criteria in both directions, then prioritizes a small, documented set of stated lifestyle wishes (quiet time, spontaneity, live music, outdoors, art or conversation), then ranks returned Qloo shared themes. Lifestyle matching is a transparent prototype rule, not unrestricted semantic understanding or a romantic compatibility score. Unrecognized wishes remain in your profile but may not affect ranking. Updated cultural favourites change the IDs sent to Qloo for subsequent recommendations. Existing conversations remain available in People.
