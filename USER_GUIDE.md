# Using EverVibe

EverVibe helps two people turn the things they love into something to do, discuss and remember together. You can bring your own interests or explore four fictional couples' stories. The experience opens in English; choose English or Simplified Chinese in **Settings**.

## Start with your own Vibe

Open **Try your own Vibe** from the home page, or visit `/try.html`.

For a quick start, select **Try two different tastes**. It looks up *Knives Out* and *Amélie*, selects New York City and fills two sample Vibes. Review the sharing checkbox and press **Start together**. You enter the same three conversation spaces as the stories: private EVE chat, direct chat, and **Make plans**. Qloo's shared themes and an editable first question are in **Make plans**.

1. **Choose something you love.** Search for a film, artist or book, then add an actual search result to **Your Vibe**. You can select up to three interests.
2. **Open a demo connection.** The black box introduces Anya, a fictional sample partner, with her photograph and interests. Her sample film is added only if the catalog returns the matching title and year. You can open **Edit the sample Vibe yourself** to change her interests or add a note about what she would enjoy today.
3. **Start together.** Choose a city, or click **Use my location** and grant browser permission for nearby ideas. Select **Share these Vibes with EVE for this session**, then press **Start together**. If a step is missing, the page explains what to do and focuses that control. Setup folds away; the space's **Settings** button reopens it, including the sharing choice.
4. **Try all three spaces.** Under **Chat**, use **Private chat with EVE** for live personal advice, or **Chat with Anya** to write directly. Use the **Perspective** buttons to switch to Anya and enter her reply. Each person has a separate EVE thread; direct chat has only the messages you enter, with no automated partner replies. **Make plans** contains the shared Qloo-assisted conversation. For example, one person can say “I'd love to discover some art” and the other “One stop sounds good. I'd like time to talk afterwards.” You write both sides; Anya is not another person online.
5. **Choose a shared idea.** Ask EVE to help plan. Open **Why these suggestions?** to see the cultural starting points. Select a candidate and confirm from both roles. You can ask a follow-up about that choice without clearing either confirmation. Ask explicitly for new or different recommendations when you want to replace it.

The black box always introduces the same fictional character. Your interests influence cultural planning; they do not search for or rank real romantic partners. Qloo provides the cultural candidates, and EVE connects them to what the two people have expressed.

Choosing a place saves an idea for this session. It does not book a table, buy a ticket or create an order.

You can ask private EVE for help even before sharing Vibes. The partner's selected interests and shared mood note enter that request only after starting the shared session. Private transcripts are never forwarded to direct chat or shared planning. **Write to Anya** opens the direct composer without sending EVE's advice. Switching spaces, roles or interface language keeps messages and separate drafts during this page session. Closing a pending EVE window cancels that request; its message remains and can be retried.

## Explore the four stories

Choose **Four stories** or visit `/stories.html`. The chapter controls select a story, and the two names switch whose view you are experiencing. Only one person's view is shown at a time.

| City | Couple | Their story |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tokyo | Kenji and Anya | A photograph, a song and the curiosity to ask about them. |
| New York City | Chloe and Sarah | Different tastes become an outing with room to hear each other. |
| London | Emma and Arjun | Everyday kindness, a romantic gesture, and learning what each person actually needs. |
| Wellington | Leo and Julian | Film nights, photography and wandering conversations become a shared way of discovering the world. |

In Tokyo, open the black box and express interest as each person before their direct conversation begins. London starts after the argument: talk it through and confirm the agreement as both people before making a plan. In Wellington, private preparation leads to words the people acknowledge saying in person.

The characters, photographs and story events are fictional. They let you experience different moments in a relationship without needing a second person to be online.

## Three conversation spaces

**Private EVE chat** is where the current person can explore a thought, ask for help or find a way to begin. **Find the words** offers an optional story-line draft: it goes into the human's composer, and you choose whether to send it. Free-text EVE conversation is also available; arbitrary wording does not necessarily complete an authored story milestone.

**Direct chat** contains the two people's messages. EVE suggestions stay in the EVE windows. Switch roles to write the other person's reply.

**Make plans** is the shared conversation with EVE. Each person can express what they want from the time together. EVE uses the shared interests and current wishes to suggest cultural possibilities. Both people choose whether an idea becomes their plan.

The story's **Our common ground** card shows each person's cultural starting points and Qloo's shared themes. These themes help shape the actual recommendation query; they are not a relationship score. Saved Qloo comparisons are labelled. A location request occurs only when you choose **Use my location**. Declining permission keeps manual city selection available; enabling location does not automatically discover or label your city.

## Sharing and EVE suggestions

Each story role has two settings:

- **Share my Vibe** makes that role's profile interests available for the relationship experience.
- **Receive EVE suggestions** allows that role to receive EVE's advice.

The shared EVE experience needs both connected people to participate. Later story chapters begin with sharing enabled as part of their fictional relationship; you can change the settings.

With both settings enabled for both people, a small wish expressed in one person's private EVE chat may inspire a short EVE suggestion in the other person's private window. It is a separate suggestion, not a copy of the conversation or a message sent as the person. It does not appear in direct chat or shared planning. EVE may also offer an in-app check-in without waiting for a new question.

The implementation screens explicit confidentiality requests, sensitive details and surprises; it can return no partner suggestion. These checks do not guarantee that every expression will be understood correctly. Turning participation off stops new delivery; a suggestion already read cannot be made unseen.

## Plans and memories

After choosing a story plan, switch roles so each person can confirm it. **After our time together** then advances the fictional timeline. It does not detect a real outing.

In **Our memories**, each person chooses the words they want to keep. Review and confirm the shared entry from both roles. The memory tree gives those moments a place to return to. You can also add a moment yourself; the app does not infer that an event happened in real life.

When both people have shared new messages and enabled the shared EVE experience, **EVE · Keep this moment?** offers **Review draft**. This draft quotes their shared words; it does not invent a memory or read private chats. You can edit it, choose a date, and save it, or select **Not now**. An unfinished personal draft is preserved. The same suggested moment earns water and nourishment only once, even when both roles review it.

## Language and session progress

Use **Settings** to change the system language at any time. The interface and authored story text change without clearing the current conversation or actions. Existing typed messages and generated responses keep their original language; new EVE responses use the selected language. Qloo's official names and addresses are kept as returned.

Your language preference is remembered on the device. Chats, selected plans, memories and story progress are held in the current page and reset on reload. **Reset this session** in Try starts that experience again. The two pages have their own session progress.

## If something is unavailable

- **No search result:** try another title or name. If Anya's sample film cannot load, her profile and the link to the stories remain available; edit her sample Vibe or retry the search.
- **Expired idea details:** clear the choice and ask for fresh ideas. The conversation stays in place; an expired reference does not silently generate a replacement.
- **Expired interests:** remove the affected selections, search again and add fresh results.
- **An interrupted reply:** use **Try again** when available. You can cancel an in-progress response; your message remains in the conversation.
- **No suitable recommendation:** change the requested activity or add another interest. The app does not invent a venue to fill the gap.
- **Live services unavailable:** authored stories remain viewable, but a service error is not a new Qloo result. A local installation needs server-side provider configuration as described in the [README](README.md).

This is a single-browser prototype, not a real multi-user dating service. It does not make purchases, reservations or external messages, and returned places do not confirm current availability or venue facilities. The app's session behavior does not determine the separate data-processing terms of its API providers or hosting service.
